How it Works

Simple to Buy. Even simpler to Use.

1

Buy the Guide

Purchase or Gift your Hidden Gem pass for $100. You'll receive the physical book, your key to the restaurants in the guide.

2

Choose a Restaurant

Browse the 20 picks in the guide. Each listing shows the restaurant, neighborhood, cuisine, and exactly what your $25 credit covers, no surprises.

3

Show Up & Enjoy

Book directly with the restaurant mentioning you have the guide. At the end of your meal, receive a stamp or signature in the guide in exchange for your dining credit.

Redemption

Your pass is valid at each of the 20 restaurants once. Come back as often as you like on your own, but the credit applies to your first visit only. Valid through December 31, 2026.

Each redemption takes $25 off your total check. You must dine with at least one other person and purchase at least 2 entrees. No minimum spend, no category restrictions. Works for two people or a full table.

Transferable

Your pass can be gifted or shared. Perfect for a partner or a food-obsessed friend. One pass, one set of credits.

No Gimmicks
